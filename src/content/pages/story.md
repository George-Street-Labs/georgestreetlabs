## It started with a dinner table

George Street Labs began with a simple observation: the best moments in life — a great meal, a summer concert, a collection you love — deserve better software than they get.

We're a small workshop by design. Every product on our board began as a real problem someone in our lives actually had, and each one has to earn its stamp before it ships.

## What we believe

- **Private where it counts.** Personal data should live on your hardware, not our servers.
- **No friction by default.** If someone can't be using it in ten seconds, it's not done.
- **Excellent over average.** When there's a choice between "it works" and "it's crafted," we choose crafted.
