By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use ofinformation in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree withour policies and practices, please do not use the App. This Privacy Policy isincorporated into and forms part of our Terms of Service.

George Street Labs LLC is the entity responsible for the processing ofyour personal information described in this Privacy Policy (the "datacontroller" for purposes of applicable data protection law), unless statedotherwise. Our contact details are set out in Section 14 ("ContactUs") below.

# 2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide to usdirectly, information we collect automatically when you use the App, andinformation we receive from third parties.

## 2.1 Information You Provide to Us

•    Account information: name, email address, username,password, profile photo, and (if applicable) phone number when you register foran account, including via third-party sign-in services such as Apple, Google,or Facebook.

•    Captures and content: photos, videos, LiDAR and depthdata, point clouds, meshes, textures, 3D models, floor plans, and other contentyou capture, upload, import, edit, or generate using the App ("UserContent").

•    Communications: information you provide when youcontact customer support, respond to surveys, or otherwise correspond with us.

•    Payment information: if you purchase a subscription orin-app product, our third-party payment processors (e.g., Apple App Store,Google Play, Stripe) collect your billing details. We do not directly storefull payment card numbers.

•    Content you choose to publish: if you elect to publishUser Content publicly (for example, to a community gallery or shared link),that content, and information associated with it (such as your username andlocation tags you add), may be visible to other users and the public.

## 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

•    Device and technical information: device model,operating system and version, unique device identifiers, IP address, mobilenetwork information, browser type, and app version.

•    Usage data: features used, scans created, sessionlength, crash logs, diagnostics, and interactions with the App.

•    Camera and sensor data: with your permission, we accessyour device's camera, LiDAR sensor, microphone, and photo library to enablecapture, scanning, and content-import features.

•    Cookies and similar technologies: we and our serviceproviders use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies on our websiteand app to recognize your device, remember preferences, and understand how theApp is used. See Section 2.4 below.

## 2.3 Information We Receive from Third Parties

•    Analytics, crash-reporting, and advertising partners,who provide us with aggregated or device-level analytics about app performanceand usage.

•    Business partners, such as enterprise customers whoinvite you to collaborate within a shared workspace.

## 2.4 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, SDKs, and similar tracking technologies tooperate and secure our website and app, remember your preferences, analyzeusage, and, where permitted, support advertising. You can control cookiesthrough your browser settings, and, on mobile, through your device's trackingand privacy settings (e.g., Apple's App Tracking Transparency or your Androidprivacy controls). •    Delete personal information we have collected from you,subject to certain exceptions;

•    Correct inaccurate personal information;

To exercise these rights, you or your authorized agent may contact ususing the details in Section 14. We will verify your request using informationassociated with your account before responding.
