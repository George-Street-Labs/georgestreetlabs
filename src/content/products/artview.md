A private, local-first gallery app — photograph your collection, present it museum-grade, and share nothing with the cloud. Built for collectors, galleries, and artists who want their works documented beautifully and kept entirely under their own control: gallery-grade capture and enhancement, elegant viewing modes for guests, and direct device-to-device sync with no accounts and no servers.
