Sweep turns your iPhone's LiDAR scanner into a professional floor-plan tool. Walk through a space, and Sweep builds an accurate 2D floor plan and 3D model as you go — no measuring tape, no CAD software, no guesswork.

Scan a room, then the next, and the next Sweep tracks doorways as you walk between rooms and snaps them together automatically, so a multi-room scan reads as one connected floor plan instead of a pile of disconnected shapes. A live quality score coaches you toward a complete, accurate scan — full coverage, steady movement, the right distance from the walls — so you know before you finish whether it's good enough to hand to a client.

Fix mistakes without rescanning LiDAR occasionally misreads a mirror as a wall, or a built-in as a doorway. Sweep's floor plan editor lets you correct it directly: drag a corner into place, relabel an opening, remove a phantom wall — all without walking the room again.

Export in the format you actually need

Floor plan as PDF or PNG, ready to share or print

CAD drawing (DXF) for AutoCAD, Revit, or SketchUp

3D walls (OBJ) for Blender and other 3D tools

3D model (USDZ) for AR Quick Look and Apple's ecosystem

Room-by-room quantities (CSV) — areas, wall area, door and window counts — ready to drop into an estimate or spreadsheet

Paint, flooring, and trim estimates calculated from your actual scan

Compare two scans side by side

A 360° tour of each room, captured as you scan

Every measurement labeled clearly, with LiDAR's accuracy stated plainly — Sweep never claims more precision than the sensor provides

Sweep requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad (iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro/Pro Max models, or a LiDAR-equipped iPad Pro).
