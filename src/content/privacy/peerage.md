  <h2 id="privacy">Privacy Policy</h2><p class="meta">Effective November 19, 2026</p>

  <h3 id="p-1">1. Who we are</h3>

  <p>Peerage is a game made and published by George Street Labs LLC (&quot;George Street Labs&quot;, &quot;we&quot;, &quot;us&quot;). This policy explains what information the Peerage app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Peerage app on iPhone, iPad and Android.</p>

  <p>Contact: <a href="mailto:info@georgestreetlabs.com">info@georgestreetlabs.com</a>. Postal address: George Street Labs LLC, 3023 N Clark St. Ste 151, Chicago, IL 60657, USA.</p>

  <h3 id="p-2">2. The short version</h3>

  <ul><li>Peerage has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.</li><li>The game contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies&#x27; apps or websites.</li><li>Your progress and settings are stored on your device.</li><li>The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve the game. You can switch this off in Settings › Share play data.</li><li>Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple (or Google on Android). We never receive your payment details.</li></ul>

  <h3 id="p-3">3. Information the app handles</h3>

  <p>Information stored only on your device. Your saved game, your choices in the story, your settings, and whether you have turned Voice on are kept in the app&#x27;s storage on your device. We cannot see this information. It is removed when you delete the app.</p>

  <p>Gameplay statistics. Unless you switch it off, the app sends the following to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC:</p>

  <ul><li>which episodes are started and finished, and whether the season is completed;</li><li>which choice is selected in a scene, recorded as the scene&#x27;s internal code and the position of the choice, and when a locked choice is tapped;</li><li>how stealth levels and mini-games turn out (for example completed, spotted, won or lost);</li><li>when the purchase screen is shown, and whether Voice is turned on or off, including whether the narration download succeeded or failed;</li><li>which screens of the app are opened, the length and number of play sessions, and the first time the app is opened;</li><li>technical details supplied automatically by the device: app version, device model, operating system version, language, and the general region (country and city level) inferred from the network address. Google does not store the full network (IP) address in Google Analytics;</li><li>a randomly generated identifier for this installation of the app (an &quot;app-instance ID&quot;), which lets the statistics from one installation be counted together. It is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.</li></ul>

  <p>These statistics do not include your name, email address, Apple Account or Google account, contacts, precise location, or anything you type. We have disabled the collection of advertising identifiers (Apple&#x27;s IDFA and the Android advertising ID) in the app, and we do not combine the statistics with information from any other source to identify you.</p>

  <p>Purchases. If you buy the season, the purchase is processed by Apple through your Apple Account, or by Google through Google Play on Android. We receive confirmation from the store, on your device, that the purchase exists so the game can unlock the episodes. We do not receive your name, card number or billing address. Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically record that an in-app purchase took place, with the product name and price, as part of the anonymous statistics described above.</p>

  <p>Game Center. If you are signed in to Apple&#x27;s Game Center, the game reports the achievements you earn and your best score in The Shoot to Game Center so they appear on your Game Center profile and leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple&#x27;s privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center, or change who can see your profile, in your device&#x27;s Settings.</p>

  <p>Narration download. If you turn Voice on, the app downloads its narration recordings from Apple&#x27;s servers. As with any download, Apple&#x27;s servers receive your device&#x27;s network address. We do not receive it.</p>

  <p>Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use them only to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence.</p>

  <ul><li>to understand how the game is played, for example where players stop, which rooms are too hard and which choices are popular;</li><li>to find and fix faults, such as a failed narration download;</li><li>to measure how many people play and on which devices, so we can decide what to build and test;</li><li>to unlock what you have bought and to restore purchases;</li><li>to answer you when you contact us.</li></ul>

  <p>We do not use any information from the app for advertising, for building profiles of individual players, or for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.</p>
