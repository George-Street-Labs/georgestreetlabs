# Privacy Policy for Habit

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the Habit mobile application (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and published by George Street Labs LLC ("George Street Labs," "we," "us," or "our").

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## The Short Version

Habit is designed to be private by default. **All of your data — your goals, settings, and activity history — is stored only on your own device.** We do not operate servers that receive your data, we do not require an account, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. We cannot see your activity in the App.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be clear about what the App does **not** do:

* We do **not** collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* We do **not** require you to create an account or log in.
* We do **not** use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.
* We do **not** transmit your goals, activity logs, reminders, or settings to us or to any third party.
* We do **not** access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device so that it can function:

* **Goals and settings**

   you configure (for example, your target, set size, duration, active hours, inactivity threshold, and reminder style).
* **Activity history**

  , meaning the records of activity you log within the App.

This information is kept in the App's local storage on your device. It remains under your control and is not sent to us. If you delete the App, this locally stored information is removed from your device along with it.

## Notifications

The App can send you reminder notifications. To do this, the App asks your device's operating system for permission to send notifications and schedules reminders through the operating system's built-in notification system. The content of these reminders is generated on your device. We do not receive or process any information in connection with these notifications. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's settings.

## Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

* **Notifications**

   — to deliver reminder alerts you have configured.
* **Vibration**

   — to provide vibration-based reminders when you select that alert style.

These permissions are used solely to provide the features described. You may decline or revoke them through your device settings, though some reminder features may not work if you do.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information at all, it does not gather such information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us; however, note that the App is designed so that no personal information is transmitted to us.

## Data Security

Because your information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, the security of that information depends primarily on the security of your device. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode, biometric lock, or other security features offered by your device's operating system.

## Third-Party Services

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store. Your download and use of the App through these platforms is subject to the respective platform's terms and privacy policies. These platforms may collect information (such as download and purchase records) independently of the App, in accordance with their own policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of these platforms.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the App or through the App Store listing as appropriate. Your continued use of the App after any change indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
