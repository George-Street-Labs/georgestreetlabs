## Privacy Policy — Sweep

**George Street Labs LLC**
Effective date: July 10, 2026
Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how George Street Labs LLC ("George Street Labs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Sweep mobile application (also listed as "Sweep: 3D Room Scanner") (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

### 1. The Short Version

Sweep is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not require an account, we do not collect your scans, we do not sell data, and we do not use advertising or tracking technologies. Your room scans, floor plans, measurements, and annotations are stored locally on your device and leave it only when you choose to export or share them.

### 2. Information Processed by the App

**Camera and LiDAR data.** The App uses your device's camera and LiDAR scanner to measure rooms and generate floor plans and 3D models. This data is processed on your device using Apple's ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks. Camera imagery and depth data are never transmitted to us.

**Photos captured by the App.** Two optional features capture photographs of your surroundings while you scan: 360° room tours, and "micro photos" — small images taken automatically as each area is measured, serving as a visual record of the scan. These photos are stored only on your device, alongside the scan they belong to, and leave your device only when you choose to share or export them. Both features can be turned off in the App's settings, and deleting a scan deletes its photos. Because these photographs depict the interior of the scanned property, take care when sharing them.

**Scan outputs.** Floor plans, 3D models, measurements, room names, annotations, and any property address you choose to enter are stored in the App's local storage on your device. We have no access to them. A property address you enter may appear on exports you generate (such as PDF floor plans); review exports before sharing.

**Diagnostics log.** The App keeps a small on-device activity log (event names and timing information only — no scans, no photos, no measurements, no addresses) to help diagnose problems. It is capped at roughly 36 KB, never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it from the Diagnostics screen, and can be cleared by you at any time.

**Preferences.** Settings such as your preferred unit system (feet/inches or meters) and whether you have completed onboarding are stored locally on your device.

**Information we do NOT collect.** The App does not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos outside the App, advertising identifiers, or analytics about your usage. The App contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

### 3. Apple Frameworks and Platform Data

The App is built on Apple frameworks (including ARKit, RoomPlan, SceneKit, and SwiftUI). Apple may process certain data as part of operating your device and these frameworks under Apple's own privacy policies, which we do not control. If you download the App through the App Store, Apple may collect information as described in Apple's privacy policy. Crash and diagnostic data may be shared with us only if you have opted into sharing diagnostics with app developers in your device settings; such data is aggregated and does not include your scans.

### 4. Sharing and Exports Initiated by You

When you export or share a file (for example, a PDF floor plan, PNG image, DXF, OBJ, USDZ, CSV, JSON file, or a photo captured by the App), that file is transferred using the destination you select (such as Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or a cloud storage provider). Once shared, the file is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service and the recipients you choose. We recommend reviewing shared files, including annotations, before sending them to third parties.

### 5. Optional iCloud Sync

The App offers an optional iCloud sync setting. When you enable it, your scan files are stored in your personal iCloud Drive account so they can sync across your devices and be restored. This storage is provided by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's terms and privacy policy; your scans are not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by George Street Labs LLC. Disabling the setting moves your scans back to local device storage. Separately, scans stored locally are included in your device's standard iCloud or local device backups, which are governed by Apple's policies.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control retention. You may delete any scan from within the App, and deleting the App removes all App data from your device (subject to any device backups you maintain, such as iCloud or local backups, which are governed by Apple's policies).

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below.

### 8. Security

Scan data resides in the App's sandboxed storage provided by iOS. We rely on the security architecture of the device and operating system. No method of storage is guaranteed to be perfectly secure; you are responsible for securing your device (e.g., with a passcode) and for the security of any destination to which you export files.

### 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA, the EU/UK under the GDPR, or other jurisdictions), you may have rights regarding personal information, such as rights of access, deletion, correction, and portability. Because we do not collect or hold your personal information on our systems, these rights are generally satisfied by the App's design: your data is in your possession, on your device. If you believe we hold any personal information about you, or wish to exercise any right, contact us at the address below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

**For EU/UK users:** to the extent any processing occurs, the legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (providing the App's functionality) and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the App. We do not transfer your personal data internationally because we do not collect it.

### 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, by in-App notice. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
