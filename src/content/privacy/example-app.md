with what your application actually does, then delete this line. Have counsel

## What this policy covers

This policy applies to the **Example App** mobile application published by
George Street Labs LLC. Other George Street Labs applications each have their
own policy — see the [full list of our privacy policies](/privacy/).

## What we collect

- **Content you create.** Describe what the app stores and where it lives — on
  the device, in iCloud, on our servers.
- **Diagnostics.** Describe any crash or usage reporting, and whether it can be
  turned off.
- **What we do not collect.** Be specific; this is the part people read.

## How the information is used

Explain each purpose the data serves, and say plainly if it is never sold,
rented, or used for advertising.

## Third parties

List every service that receives data — analytics, crash reporting, payment
processing, cloud sync — and link to each one's own policy. Say "none" if that
is the case.

## Data retention and deletion

Say how long data is kept and how someone deletes it, including what happens
when the app is removed from the device.

## Children

State the intended audience and whether the app knowingly collects anything
from children under 13.

## Your rights
